## Study information
**Title.** Prospective evaluation of a hierarchical ANFIS (HANFIS) for dynamic exploitability-exposure scoring of container images.

**Description.** HANFIS is a three-panel hierarchical Sugeno ANFIS (34 rules, 176 parameters) that scores container images from seven inputs (CVSS, exploitability = EPSS x runtime reachability, patch risk, MFA risk, Kubernetes exposure, asset criticality, threat intelligence).
Earlier confirmatory work (E4, protocol committed 2026-09-30, tag `e4-prereg-2026-09-29`) found that HANFIS beats a CVSS-only score, a linear model, and a parameter-matched flat ANFIS, but is not distinguishable from a 4-week persistence baseline.
A post hoc analysis of that data suggested that HANFIS is reactive: it helps when the label moves and not when it is stable. This registration tests that suggestion prospectively on weeks whose labels have not been computed. A further post hoc analysis found that a four-parameter formula of two inputs (the EPSS-based exploitability input and the KEV indicator) has a lower error than HANFIS and than persistence on out-of-fold data; the registration also tests this formula prospectively.

**Hypotheses.**
- **P7a (regime).** In forward weeks without a change of EPSS model version, HANFIS is not better than persistence: the upper bound of the 95% CI of its skill score (SS) against persistence is below 0.05.
- **P7b (hybrid).** A fixed blend of HANFIS and persistence, `y_hat = 0.5109 * y_HANFIS + 0.5891 * y_lag4`, beats persistence: SS >= 0.05 and the lower 95% bound > 0.
- **P7d (information beyond persistence; secondary).** The mean weekly Spearman correlation between predicted change (`y_HANFIS - y_lag4`) and realised change (`y - y_lag4`) has a lower 95% bound > 0.
- **P7e (second family; post hoc origin).** A four-parameter formula `y_hat = a + b * max(x2, c * x7)` (x2 = the exploitability input, x7 = the KEV indicator, both at time t), fitted once on the same training rows as the HANFIS models and then frozen (`protocols/E7_formula_2026-10-12.json`), beats persistence: SS >= 0.05 and the lower 95% bound > 0.
- **P7f (second family; post hoc origin).** HANFIS is not better than that formula: the upper bound of the 95% CI of the HANFIS skill score against the formula is below 0.05 (refuted if SS >= 0.05 with a lower bound > 0).
- P7c (precision at 20% on levels) is descriptive only: persistence already reaches 0.969 on earlier data, a ceiling effect that makes it uninformative.

## Design plan
Prospective, observational, no intervention. Models are frozen before the outcome is computed: five HANFIS models (fixed seeds) retrained on all labelled weeks up to 2026-07-27 (the first forward week, 2026-08-31, minus a 30-day embargo, as in the earlier cross-validation, so that no training label window overlaps the forward period); model file hashes [HASH LIST]; the blend weight is fixed in `protocols/E7_lambda_2026-10-08.json` (fitted on out-of-fold E4 predictions only). The formula coefficients are fixed in `protocols/E7_formula_2026-10-12.json` (fitted on the same training rows as the models; SHA256 [FORMULA HASH]).

## Sampling plan
**Existing data.** Registration prior to accessing the outcome data: the weekly EPSS snapshots after 2026-09-21 have not been downloaded (one CISA KEV catalog snapshot dated 2026-09-28 was fetched earlier for another analysis and is not used to compute forward labels) and no label for weeks >= 2026-08-31 has been computed. Inputs for 2026-08-31 .. 2026-09-21 exist in the project dataset with null labels; inputs for later weeks do not exist.
**Data collection.** Public EPSS and CISA KEV archives, sealed with a manifest of hashes; container images are digest-pinned (120 images, same inventory as E4) and rescanned with a vulnerability database frozen on the data date D2.
**Sample.** 120 images x forward weeks t with t + 28 days <= D2. Primary D2 = 2026-11-02 (6 weeks: 2026-08-31 .. 2026-10-05); fallback D2 = 2026-11-09 only if fewer than 6 weeks are usable when the dataset is built, decided before any model is run on forward data. Fewer than 6 usable weeks: the study is postponed, not relaxed.

## Variables
**Outcome.** Realised exploitability exposure y(a,t) = (100/30) x the mean over weekly snapshots tau in {t+7, ..., t+28 days} of the maximum over active CVEs v of X_v(tau) x rho_v, with X_v = max(EPSS, KEV indicator) and rho_v the runtime-reachability factor (definition frozen in the E4 protocol, amendment A-13).
**Predictors and comparators.** HANFIS (mean of five seeds), persistence (label of week t-4), hybrid (above), ridge on the seven inputs (descriptive), and the four-parameter formula (above).

## Analysis plan
Pooled mean squared error on the raw 0-100 scale; SS = 1 - MSE_model / MSE_persistence. Cluster bootstrap by image (2,000 resamples, seed 20261102, percentile CI). Holm correction over {P7a, P7b} at one-sided alpha 0.025 (thresholds 0.0125 and 0.025). Decision rules as stated in the hypotheses. P7e and P7f form a second family with its own Holm correction (one-sided alpha 0.025; thresholds 0.0125 and 0.025), controlled separately from {P7a, P7b} because the two families answer different questions; the combined one-sided error is at most 0.05. A week-level cluster bootstrap is reported as a sensitivity analysis without criteria.
**Reported without criteria.** SS per stratum of input change available at time t (4-week change of exploitability input or KEV status), per-fold blend weights, log1p scale, results without the image `wordpress`, precision at 20% and weekly Spearman on levels.
**Exclusions.** Weeks without a complete snapshot sequence; weeks after a change of EPSS model version are analysed separately as a break regime. Rows with unresolved vulnerability-range evidence (INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE) are dropped only if they are at most 2% of test-week rows; an asset that loses all of its test weeks leaves the forward analysis (it has no test rows) and is reported. At registration, `cockroachdb` has unresolved evidence for seven consecutive weeks (2026-08-10 to 2026-09-21) and is expected to leave. Beyond the 2% limit the analysis is put on hold.
**Power.** Limited: on earlier out-of-fold data the hybrid's SS was 0.174 with a 95% CI of [0.011, 0.236] and a Holm p of 0.021 (above the 0.0125 threshold). Results that are not significant will be reported as not demonstrated, not as equivalence. On historical six-week windows of out-of-fold predictions (post hoc, overlapping windows), the formula's advantage over persistence met its criterion in about one third of windows, so a non-confirmation of P7e must not be read as a refutation. The image-clustered bootstrap treats weekly shocks shared by all images as fixed; with six weeks the effective number of time points is six. In out-of-fold data (45 weeks) the week-clustered 95% CI for the formula's skill against persistence ([+0.127, +0.672]) was about three times wider than the image-clustered one ([+0.263, +0.425]); a result that meets its criterion under image-clustered inference but not under the week-clustered sensitivity will be reported as such (confirmed by image-clustered inference, fragile by week-clustered inference).

## Other
**Transparency.** Hypotheses P7a, P7b, and P7d derive from a post hoc analysis of E4 data and from a rehearsal of the analysis code on out-of-fold predictions (not on forward data). The formula and hypotheses P7e and P7f originate from post hoc analyses of the same out-of-fold data (their out-of-fold support is not counted as confirmation); the formula coefficients are fixed before any forward label is computed. The target does not depend on the three environmental inputs, so this study cannot test their value.
